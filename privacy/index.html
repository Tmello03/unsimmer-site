Unsimmer

Privacy policy

Unsimmer for iPhone and iPad. Last updated 17 November 2026.

Unsimmer has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking code. The developer receives none of your data. If Unsimmer crashes or freezes, the system's report stays on your device; nothing is sent unless you send feedback yourself.

How recipes are made

  • Links. When you save or share a link, your device downloads that page (only that address, and only public web pages). With "Photos from recipe links" on, that page's photo of the dish is downloaded too.
  • Photos and scanned pages are read on your device.
  • Messy or dictated recipes are tidied by Apple Intelligence on your device, or by your own AI key if you added one.
  • Nutrition and cost estimates come from tables built into the app, so nothing about your recipes is sent anywhere for them.

Cooking

  • Recipe timers use your device's alarms.
  • Cook mode can read steps aloud and, if you turn on hands-free, listen for words like "next" or "start the timer". Both use Apple's speech on your device, only while Cook mode is open, and keep nothing.
  • Tapping a saved store opens Apple Maps with the store's name. Unsimmer does not ask for your location.

If you also use Unspool

Unsimmer and Unspool (by the same developer) share the same iCloud space, so your recipes, Family book and shopping list are the same in both. Unsimmer only reads and changes the recipe side. Unspool's other notes, reminders and people arrive with that iCloud data and are discarded as they arrive: Unsimmer never keeps, shows, changes or sends them.

Purchases

Unsimmer Plus is bought through Apple. The developer never sees your payment details or Apple ID.

What we don't do

  • No selling or sharing of your data, and no advertising.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting services or third-party tracking code.
  • No account, email address or phone number is asked for.

Your control

You can delete recipes at any time, turn off iCloud sync in Unsimmer's settings, remove your AI key, and change any permission (camera, microphone, speech recognition, alarms, notifications) in iOS Settings → Unsimmer. Deleting the app removes its data from your device; data in your iCloud account can be managed in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud.

Children

Unsimmer is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
