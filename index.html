Privacy policy

Unsimmer

A recipe book that cooks with you, for iPhone and iPad. Save a recipe from a link, a photo of a cookbook page or your own words, then cook it step by step with timers, a shopping list sorted by aisle and a meal plan for the week.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas: Trevorjmell3@proton.me. Please say which device and iOS version you use, and what you were doing.

Good to know

  • No account is needed, and the developer receives none of your recipes. See the privacy policy.
  • Recipes are read and tidied on your device. Messy or dictated recipes use Apple Intelligence where your device has it (iPhone 15 Pro or newer, or an iPad with an M-series chip), or your own AI key under Settings (optional, off by default).
  • If iCloud sync is on (the default), your recipes and shopping list sync through your own iCloud account. If you also use Unspool, both apps share the same recipes.
  • Cook mode, timers, the shopping list and the meal plan are free. Unsimmer Plus is a one-time purchase that adds hands-free cooking, ingredient swaps, the Family recipe book and more, and works with Family Sharing.
  • Recipe timers use your device's alarms, so they ring even when the app is closed or the phone is locked.

Shared recipe links

When someone shares a recipe with "Share as web page", the whole recipe travels inside the link and opens on this site's recipe page, with ticks and working timers, in any browser. No server receives the recipe.
